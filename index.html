Questions

Is it safe for children?

Kids mode only offers gentle topics. Ghost and horror genres cannot be chosen there, and the library refuses to store a scary story on a child profile.

Does it cost anything?

Early access is free, including 5-minute stories. Premium (US$5.99) and Family (US$9.99) are planned; no payments are taken today.

Is there anything for parents?

After you sign in, switch to For me for grown-up sleep stories. Those stories are not shown in kids mode.
